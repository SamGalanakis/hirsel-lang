# External oracles

- **CONC-1:** instrument runtimes to ensure suspension occurs only at Io/capability calls and cancellation checks. Check fairness among tasks that repeatedly reach those points with a scheduler model. Compare thread, pool, cooperative and simulated implementations; do not assert a fixed interleaving or unconditional termination. A loop without suspension may starve other tasks, as the rule explicitly permits.
- **CONC-2:** compare simulator traces to a structured-task model: inline starts stop at first suspension, normal exits join, error/failure exits cancel then join, and detached children still join before exit. The bounded @blocking sync pool is a scheduler/resource oracle. The corpus has a detached-child output-order witness and handle-consumption controls.
- **CONC-3:** use cancellation injection at every blocking operation and effect boundary, then check repeated cancellation, no-effect-on-Canceled, loan duration until completion, shielded drops, joins and mutex acquisition, and deadlines driven by Io clock. Finite programs cannot prove all injection points obey these properties.
- **CONC-4:** a sequential-consistency model must explain spawn/start, completion/join/scope-exit, send/receive, release/acquire, atomic, and suspend/resume traces. Stress the RC runtime on weak-memory targets, checking task-local counts and atomic Shared roots. Race freedom is a mode/checker theorem, not a claim established by one execution.
- **CONC-5:** lock runtime instrumentation verifies bodies run synchronously in the acquiring task, with plain payload counts serialised by the lock. Combine the authority checker model with scheduling traces to establish lock-order deadlock exclusion; body divergence is not forbidden.
- **CONC-6:** use the channel/RC model to prove dropping the final Receiver releases all buffered values and breaks cycles through Sender-containing payloads. Finite executions can observe resource drops, but ordinary freeing is deliberately unobservable. Stress endpoint drop/send races against the sequentially consistent model.
- **CONC-7:** scalar atomic runtime traces must match sequential consistency and Tasks scheduling events. A single-task store/load witness does not prove multicore ordering.
